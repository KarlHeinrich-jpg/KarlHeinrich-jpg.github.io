## 🇬🇧 About Me

I'm an undergraduate studying **Financial Engineering** at the **School of Economics, Beijing Technology and Business University (BTBU)**. My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning**, **stochastic processes**, and **quantitative finance** — I'm interested in how explainable AI and stochastic differential equations can improve prediction and pricing in economic and financial systems.

**Research interests:** Machine Learning · Financial Stochastic Processes · Stochastic Differential Equations · Option Pricing
