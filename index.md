# Yile Wang (王毅乐)

Welcome to my personal academic webpage! I am an academic researcher and student specializing in financial engineering, agricultural economics, and data science. 

## About Me
My research integrates complex econometric modeling and advanced data science techniques to evaluate economic policies and drive predictive analytics. I have extensive experience in causal inference, specifically employing Difference-in-Differences (DID) and Propensity Score Matching (PSM-DID) alongside robust parallel trend and placebo tests in Stata and Python. 

Additionally, I utilize machine learning ensemble frameworks—such as stacking models with XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost—paired with SHAP value interpretation for feature importance and out-of-sample robustness checks.

## Research Interests
*   **Agricultural Risk Management & Financial Engineering:** Investigating the "Insurance + Futures" model and its utility increments for agricultural products (such as sugar and hogs) to understand its impact on regional economic growth.
*   **Predictive Sensory Modeling:** Designing machine learning projects to predict sensory profiles based on chemical compound indicators, prioritizing salty flavor indicators over other sensory features in out-of-sample testing.
*   **Remote Sensing Applications:** Applying multi-task remote sensing interpretation models for land use classification, cultivated land detection, and disaster area mapping using domestic commercial satellite data.
*   **Digital Economy & Policy Evaluation:** Exploring the impact of the digital economy on total factor productivity, evaluating spatiotemporal patterns, and analyzing the relationship between Global Policy Uncertainty (GPR) and corporate cash holdings using panel data regressions and instrumental variable (IV) analysis.
