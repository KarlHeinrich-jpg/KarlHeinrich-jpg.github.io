01 / About

Rigorous methods.
Useful answers.

My work sits at the intersection of economic policy, financial risk, and computational modeling—where careful identification meets real-world complexity.

I use causal inference methods including Difference-in-Differences and PSM-DID, supported by parallel-trend, placebo, and robustness testing in Stata and Python.

For predictive work, I build ensemble systems with XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost, then use SHAP and out-of-sample validation to make their results easier to trust and explain.
